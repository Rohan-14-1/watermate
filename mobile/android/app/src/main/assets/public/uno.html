UNO Game Rules

🎯 Goal: Be the first player to get rid of all your cards.
🔄 Matching: Play a card that matches the top discard card by color or number/action.
⚡ Calling UNO: When you have 1 card left, declare UNO before playing or your turn ends!
🃏 Wild Cards: Can be played anytime to choose the next active color.
🛑 Special Cards: Skip pauses the next player, Reverse changes play direction, and Draw Two forces +2 cards.
